# Appendix D: Sensitivity analyses and computational efficiency assessment

Detailed analyses are provided in the accompanying repository, covering three aspects: (D.1) sensitivity of dynamic responses to convergence tolerance and coupling time step; (D.2) sensitivity of operational stiffness identification to window length and advance interval; and (D.3) computational efficiency and convergence of four coupling strategies, examining the contributions of state-informed predictor and IQN-ILS.
